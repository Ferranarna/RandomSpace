How to play

You start with 1000

Choose your bet with + and − (from 10 to 100) and press SPIN!

Your bet multiplies what you win or lose:
10 = x1 · 100 = x10

Victory

3 burgers = the whole jackpot, multiplied by your bet

Defeat

If your balance drops below 10 you can no longer bet: you start again with 1000

Progressive jackpot

It starts at 1000 and rises 100 with every defeat
